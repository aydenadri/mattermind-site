What it is

About twenty minutes of puzzles, mostly easy on purpose, and a few questions with no right answers. At the end, a brain drawn from what you did. It is not a verdict, it is not a diagnosis, and it is not a picture of your actual brain.

Do it in one sitting

Nothing is saved. If you close the app part way through, the session is gone and you start again. That is on purpose: how long you take is half of what it reads, and a session stitched back together has pauses in it that a fresh one does not.

If the screen locks

A few puzzles are long enough for the screen to lock if you stop to think. A lock in the middle of a timed question is read as a slow answer. For the sitting, set Auto-Lock to a longer time in Settings, under Display and Brightness.

The calm version

Tick "Calm version" on the first screen if you need a still screen. Nothing moves, and the flight at the end does not play. The questions and the brain are the same.

Using it offline

Everything is in the app. It works the same in airplane mode.
