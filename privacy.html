Mattermind

Privacy policy. Last updated 2 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Mattermind does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not track you.

How the app works

  • The whole app runs on your device. It makes no network requests.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Nothing is saved. Your answers and your timing are held in the app's memory while you play, and they are gone when you close it.
  • Nothing about your session is sent anywhere. If a later version ever offers to add your answers to a comparison group, it will ask you first, on the first screen, and this page and its date will change before it does.

What the result is

The result is something to think about. It is not a diagnosis, it is not a screening result, and it is not a picture of your actual brain. Mattermind is not a medical device.

Apple

Downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and its date will change first.
